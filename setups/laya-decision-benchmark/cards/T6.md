# T6 — Broken wikilink: real or placeholder

- **Question type:** noul
- **Required precision:** 0.95
- **Source:** broken wikilinks found at four vault snapshots (2026-08-10, 08-24, 09-07, 09-14) with
  `vault_audit.py`'s own resolver, labelled by their state at HEAD. Real = the target note exists at HEAD.
  Placeholder = the link is still unresolved at HEAD. Links that disappeared are dropped because they are
  ambiguous (fixed or deleted).
- **Result:** 98 labelled links (55 train / 14 cal / 29 test), **all placeholder**. In the snapshot
  window, no broken link was ever fixed by creating its target, so the positive class is empty.
- **Status:** **not run.** Precision and coverage are undefined without positives. Meets S3 (fewer than 40
  gold items in any usable form). The current `is_placeholder` regex stays as is.
- **What would make it runnable:** a human pass over the current 111 "real" broken links, labelling each
  as fix or placeholder. That would give an actual gold set.
